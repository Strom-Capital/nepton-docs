# How to follow an article

You can subscribe to e-mail notifications from the support service for each article when they are updated.
**We recommend that all admin users follow at least the[Platform status](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360006554777>) and [Release notes](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360006615478>) pages.** The Platform status page informs about potential interruptions in the service, and the Platform status page about new features and updates released to Nepton.
Following articles requires that you have signed up and logged in to our support service. [In this article](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4406611836817>), you can find instructions for signing up for Nepton Support.
Open the article you want to follow. Click the **Follow** button in the upper right corner. You can also unfollow from the same button.
