# Nepton Support portal overlook

Nepton Support instruction articles are divided by topic. All instructions related to how to use Nepton can be found on the front page:
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/23100017681181)
The instructions have been divided under four categories;  
-Support portal  
-Platform status and versions  
-Support  
-News and events
Support portal contains articles about using the Guide, for example how to register, search for articles and send support requests. Platform status and versions includes information about the current status of the service, as well as past and upcoming updates. Under support category, you can find all guidance related to using Nepton service. News and events category includes webinars and information about other public events.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/23100017684893)
You can also search instructions by using the search function:
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/23100017688605)
The results are articles which topic contains the search word. If the drop-down menu does not offer a suitable article, you can press Enter to see more relative search results:
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/20118012657821)
We are constantly updating the Nepton Support articles and the search function. If you are a trained administrator in your organization and notice gaps or errors in the instructions, please let us know so that we can update the instructions.
We also recommend [registering](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4406611836817>) to our support site. Once logged in, articles can also be [followed](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4406604045585>). The perk of following an article comes in when the article is updated: you will get a notification to your email about the update immediately. In addition, trained administrators can send [support requests](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4406611917841>) from the support site directly to Nepton's customer service and [follow](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4416596910353-Own-service-requests>) the status of requests.
