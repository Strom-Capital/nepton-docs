# Worktime role members

You can view and edit **worktime role members** in the action menu under **worktime roles.**
**![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/29362664622109)**
  
Once you've selected the right worktime role, choose "worktime role members" as action.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/29362664624157)  

A list of all users in the service is now displayed. The members of the selected worktime role are marked by blue box in front of the name. 
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/29362664626845)  
  
If you want to add members to selected worktime role, you can tick the box in front of their name and then click **'Save'** at the bottom. Now user has been transferred from one worktime role to another.
