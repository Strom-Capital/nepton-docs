# Work contract notifications

Automatic notifications for work contracts can be managed in **Employees > Administration > Settings > Automated notifications**. You can choose which updates on work contracts will send automatic notification.
To be able to receive notifications, email address and phone number must be saved on persons info.
## Notifications can be sent on following situations:
  * After creating a first working contract
  * After creating a subsequent working contract
  * After employee accepts a working contract


![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/40385577969565)
When the service is selected by clicking the "Send an email to person", more configurations are displayed. Subject and content for email can be added. Email can also be configured to be sent to certain email addresses in addition to the persons connected to the work contract.
The email can be configured to use secure email. These services are offered by different service providers. In order for this configuration to work, the secure email sending service must be in use and the service must be defined in Nepton > Employees > Administration Settings > Change to custom email server (SMTP)
When secure email is sent, recipient will have a message that says "You have had secure email, click this link to open the email". When the link is clicked and recipient chooses "I want to see the content of message", recipient will get pin-code via text message to open it.
