# Calendar Synchronisation

Nepton's calendar can be synchronized with another calendar. Events marked on Nepton (as selected) are automatically transferred to your calendar. This feature is only available if your non-Nepton calendar supports synchronization of events from an external service.
Note that only 7 days from the past and the next 3 years of future events are synchronized.
## Enabling Calendar Synchronisation
Go to **Worktime > Events in calendar.** On the top right corner of the page, click Tools and choose Calendar Syncing. Click +Add:
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/40269743005981)
Choose which calendar you want to synchronize:  
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/40269766671261)
Choose, which event types you wish to export from Nepton:
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/40269766671773)
Click Generate link and copy the link.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/40269743015581)
## Setting the link to an external calendar 
If your calendar supports syncing events from an external calendar, you should be able to create a new calendar from your calendar with options that resemble "Import by URL" or "Import by iCal (ics)". The link you copied can be set with this function.
**Outlook**
![mceclip0.png](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/360005534497)
The added calendar and selected events should now appear on your external calendar. Events are updated on your calendar about every hour. If the update does not take place, set the link again to update the events.
**TIP** : In Outlook, you can use the Overlay feature to bring Nepton and Outlook calendar views together.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/40269780325405)
