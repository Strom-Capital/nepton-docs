# Employers

Employers contain information about personnel’s employer. This can be especially useful when managing a group of personnel having varying employers due to corporate structure. Information related to employers can be freely defined. This is done by adding related additional information fields to the employer information. You can set employers in **Employees > Employers**.
Employers-field can be used with [Visma LTR integration](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/11166111654685>).
## Managing employers
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/28250746151581)
Employer can be added by selecting "Add new employer". By default employer has "Name" and "Code" fields. Employer can also have customizable employer info, which can be added by clicking "Add employer info" button. Employer info will have a field name and a value. Employer info values are shown as hidden by default as they might contain passwords or other sensitive information.
![e1.png](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/10849249748381)
Employer can be edited by clicking edit-icon on the left of the employer in employer list.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/10847617086621)
## Adding employers for persons
Employers can be added for persons by editing their person information. Go to Employees > Persons > open the right person's details > Employment information. Choose the correct employer from the list and save the changes at the bottom of the page.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/40240987599773)
