# Adding persons to a unit

A person is added to a unit in the Employment section under Units.
The desired unit's information is opened by selecting the magnifying glass icon next to the unit's name.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/28216523336733)
The unit's information is divided into three tabs; Unit information, unit persons and additional functions.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/28216529272477)
A list of persons in the unit is shown in "unit persons". 
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/28216529274781)
To add a person, enter the name of the person to be added in the **Search for person by keyword** field and select **Search**.
The person to be added appears as a search result and is added to the unit by selecting **Add**. The person can now be seen in the **Unit persons** listing.
The unit can also be specified directly in the person's information. Several units can be assigned to a person and one of these can be assigned as the primary unit.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/19328220072605)
