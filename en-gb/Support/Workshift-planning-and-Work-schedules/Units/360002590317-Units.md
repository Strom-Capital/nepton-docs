# Units

Unit is the highest level of the organization and Locations are established under the units. If more than one unit has been assigned to the person in the Employees section, he can be added as a resource to the locations under all of these units in shift planning. This makes it possible, for example, to plan substitutions in shift planning between units.
There must be at least one unit established before locations can be established for Shift Planning.
Units are created in the Employees section. Units form the company's organizational structure.  
See how to [add units](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002590337-Adding-units>).
Several units can be assigned for a person, one of which is defined as the primary unit.  
See instructions on how to [add people to units](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002595478-Adding-persons-to-the-unit>).
