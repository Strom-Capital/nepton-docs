# Restoring a removed unit

It is possible to restore a removed unit if needed, or for example temporarily for renaming on old unit to reuse its name (see. [Reuse unit name or unit code](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4405365426833>)).
Go to **Employees > Units**. Tick the "show deleted" box in search options, and either search for all units or specify the unit's name in the search. You will receive a list of units, out of which the deleted units will have a "restore" button on their left hand side. 
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/28217207819165)
Click on this "restore" button, and the unit's is active again and it's information can be edited.
