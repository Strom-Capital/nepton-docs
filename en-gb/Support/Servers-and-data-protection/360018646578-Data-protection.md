# Data protection

Saarni Nepton Oy is the processor of personal data.

---|---|---|---|---

A general data protection statement can be found on the Nepton website: <https://nepton.com/data-privacy-statement>
We offer our customers an [internal template](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/6096229427357>) for the data protection statement regarding the use of the Nepton service.
Data protection and data security descriptions are available as part of the overall service description. You can request this document from your service provider.
Person responsible for Customer's data protection must register to Nepton support service and subscribe to this article (FOLLOW), so that he will receive notifications when new version of this article (including changes to trusted services or sub-processors) is published.
Changes to Nepton data protection and sub-processors will also be communicated in advance through other communication channels.
# Sub-processors
Personal data can be transferred to or processed by sub-processors when this is necessary for the proper functionality and development of the Nepton service. Only necessary data is transferred to or processed by sub-processors.
Saarni Nepton is responsible for ensuring the implementation of information security and data protection with regards to sub-processors. There is always a valid data protection agreement in place between Saarni Nepton and sub-processors.
