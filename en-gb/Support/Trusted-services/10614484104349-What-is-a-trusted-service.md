# What is a trusted service?

Nepton offers a comprehensive solution to a wide range of use cases and requirements. Trusted services are part of Nepton, and you can find them in the top-level navigation. By default, you have access to free lite versions of trusted services. You can explore and try out these services to understand how they can benefit you in your work.  
Trusted services are offered by companies within Saarni Cloud -group and by other trusted partners. List of trusted service providers and other personal data sub-processors is available [here](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360018646578>).
For a list of trusted services and more information about each, please see the [Trusted Services](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/sections/10402279644061-Trusted-services>) -section.
