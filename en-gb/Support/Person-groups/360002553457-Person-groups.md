# Person groups

Person groups are used to describe an organizational model or other hierarchy of persons. The visibility of projects can be limited to a certain person group. Person groups also serve as filters when taking out reports.
Person groups don't affect the calculations of worktime.
Person group settings can be found in **Worktime > Administration > Person groups.
