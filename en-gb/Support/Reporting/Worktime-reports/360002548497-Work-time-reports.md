# Work time reports

There are multiple standardized reports in Nepton. They can be found in Worktime section under **Work hours > Reports**.
Hours targeted to projects can be reported in **Projects > Reports.** More about project reporting [here](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002548957>). 
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/25732824865821)
There are different reports regarding work time, work time shortening leaves and annual leaves for example. The reports can be searched for a certain time period and filtered users. The reports can be printed and some imported into Excel. If the downloaded report contains too much data (too many users or too long of a time period), the report can only be opened in Excel. Further instructions on how to filter and take out reports can be found [here](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360004437597>).
All articles regarding work time reports can be found [here](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/sections/360005870318-Worktime-reports>). 
## Reports regarding recorded work time
Reports regarding recorded work time are useful when monitoring for example how works hours are divided between different event types, overtime compensations and absences. More in [this article](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002548557>).
## Reports regarding sick leaves
The reports regarding sick leave can be used to monitor, for example, the total number of sick leave days (working days/Kela days/calendar days), absences recorded for different reason codes and the number of paid sick leave days.
Reports regarding sick leave include:  
\- Sick leaves by person  
\- Sick leaves by person group
More in [this article](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002548797>).
## Reports regarding work time shortening leave
It is possible to activate [work time shortening leave earning](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002559837>) in Nepton. After the function is taken into use, it is possible to follow the earning and use of work time shortening leaves via reports. 
There is one standardized report regarding work time shortening leaves:  
\- Work time shortening leaves
More in [this article.](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002548777>)
## Reports regarding annual leave
It is possible to activate [annual leave earning](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002560698>) in Nepton. After the function is taken into use, it is possible to follow the earning and use of annual leaves via reports. 
Reports regarding annual leave include:  
\- Annual leave earning by month  
\- Annual leave usage by month  
\- Annual leave usage by season
More in [this article.](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002548717>)
## Reports regarding dining events
It is possible to record dining events in Nepton, just like any other events. These dining events can be monitored with the following reports:
-Dining by person  
-Dining totals by person
More in [this article.](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002548278>)
