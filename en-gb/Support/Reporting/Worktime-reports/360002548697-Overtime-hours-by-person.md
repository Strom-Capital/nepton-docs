# Overtime hours by person

Overtime hours can be tracked using the overtime hours by person report. The report can be found in the Worktime section under **Work hours > Reports > Overtime hours by person**.
**![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/27331496738717)**
### Raportin valinnat
When taking the report out, first select the time period you want to view. By clicking on the date, a calendar opens, from which you can select the start and end dates of the reporting period.
You can also choose whether to create the report with all possible columns, even if some of them are empty, or whether to only display columns for which data has been generated.
Finally, select the people whose information you want to be included in the report. The "Show report" button displays the results on the screen, and they can also be printed or exported to Excel.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/27331496740253)
Ylityötunnit henkilöittäin -raportilla näytetään raportointijakson aikana tehty ylityö jaoteltuna ylityötyypeittäin:
\- Lisätyö  
\- Vuorokautinen tai viikoittainen 50 % ylityö  
\- Vuorokautinen tai viikoittainen 100 % ylityö  
\- TES ylityö
Lisäksi tehdyt ylityötunnit näytetään raportilla korvaustyypeittäin, eli maksetaanko tunnit palkassa vai kerryttävätkö ne saldoa tai työaikapankin saldoa.
The report shows the overtime worked during the reporting period, broken down by overtime type:
\- Additional work  
\- Daily or weekly 50% overtime  
\- Daily or weekly 100% overtime  
\- TES overtime
The overtime hours are also shown in the report by compensation type, i.e. whether the hours are paid in salary or accrue a balance.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/27331787406365)
In the example picture above, the person has done overtime targeted to both salary and balance.
