# Personnel report

The personnel report is available in the Employees section under the Persons menu. The report reports personnel information by age, gender, current employment status, or unit information.  
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/27326280482589)
The start and end dates of the report are selected by clicking on the small calendar icon and selecting the timeframe from the calendar that opens. In the Chosen unit section, it is possible to limit the report to a specific unit or take it of the entire working community.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/27326280483357)
The reports provide information about both active and deleted individuals if they have had a valid employment contract during the selected time period.
### Reports on age and gender
The personnel report generates reports on the age and gender distribution of personnel. The report is based on information obtained from the personal identity code. The report is only available if the personal identity codes have been entered in the personal data.
### Work relationships Report
The work relationships report provides information on started, ended, and current contracts, if the start and end dates of the employment contracts have been entered in the personal data.
