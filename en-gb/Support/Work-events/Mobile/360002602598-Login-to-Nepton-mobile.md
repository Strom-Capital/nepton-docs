# Login to Nepton mobile

Log in to Nepton mobile at <https://go.nepton.com/>. The same address is also used in the desktop version. It's a good idea to save the [sign-in page as a shortcut](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002596437>) on your phone's desktop from the mobile view after logging in, making it easy and quick to open with a single tap. There is no app for Nepton, it is always used in browser.
On first use, the service will ask you to enter the name of your working community. Usually, the name of the working community is the name of your company. If you are not sure what to enter here, please contact your Nepton administrator or your supervisor.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/29558604591901)
Once the working community name is entered, you will be directed to next page where all enabled login methods for the working community are presented. Working communities can have different login methods in use, such as Nepton credentials, single sign-ons and RFID tags. Please note that only Microsoft and Google SSO's work with mobile, other single sign-on methods do not.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/29558604592669)
If you are using the service for the first time, you can set your password using the '**Forgot password** ' function. After entering your email address, you will receive further instructions on the email address you provided.
If the login page shows single sign-on by default, select Log in using Nepton credentials to enter your username and password.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/29558619593629)
You can also set the page to keep you logged in. Enabling this feature can be done by the administrator in the [service settings](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/13194906030365>).
