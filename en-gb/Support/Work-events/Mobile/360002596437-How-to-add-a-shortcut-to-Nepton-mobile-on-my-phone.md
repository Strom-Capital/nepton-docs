# How to add a shortcut to Nepton mobile on my phone?

It's a good idea to save the log in page as a shortcut on your phone's home screen, making it easy and quick to open with a single tap. How to add a webpage as a shortcut to the home screen depends on your phone model. The view in doing this might also change depending on the browser version and your phone's personal settings.
## Adding a shortcut in Chrome
1\. [**Login to Nepton.**](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002602598>)
2\. In the **mobile view** below, click the button with **three dots** on the right side of the address-bar.  
**![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/21314354171677)**  

3\. From the opening menu, click "**Add to Home screen** "
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/21314316958109)
4\. Click "**Install** ".
5\. If the shortcut does not appear on the home screen, find the ‘Nepton’ app among your installed apps and add the app’s shortcut to the home screen.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/21314316962333)
## Adding a shortcut using Microsoft Edge
1\. [**Login to Nepton.**](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002602598>)
2\. In the **mobile view** below, open a menu from the button on the bottom of the view.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/21314354179613)
3\. Click "**Add to phone** ".
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/21311714358813)
## Adding a shortcut on an IPhone/IOS device
1\. [**Login to Nepton.**](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002602598>)
2\. In the **mobile view** below, click the **Share-button** at the bottom of the view.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/21311788130205)
3\. Swipe the menu up to see the lower part of the menu with additional options.
4\. Click "**Add to Home Screen** ".  
![mceclip1.png](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/360018348777)
