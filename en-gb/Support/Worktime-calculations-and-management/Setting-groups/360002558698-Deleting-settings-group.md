# Deleting settings group

Settings groups can also be deleted. If a person’s settings group is deleted, the person’s calculations for the entire period they were in the deleted settings group will be removed.
We always recommend creating a new settings group and then, instead of deleting, modifying the name of the settings group, e.g., ‘Hourly Workers’ -> ‘Hourly Workers NOT IN USE’. This way, the person’s calculations will also be preserved in the past. You can find instructions for modifying the settings group [here](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002558758-Edit-the-name-code-and-description-of-a-setting-group>).
A settings group can be deleted as follows: Select **Worktime > Administration > Setting groups > Setting Groups: Group to be deleted > Delete Settings Group **
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/21132039022109)  
The service will confirm if you are sure. Select **Yes**. The service will then confirm the deletion of the settings group.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/21132039026589)
