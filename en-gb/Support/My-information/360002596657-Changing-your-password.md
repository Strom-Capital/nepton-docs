# Changing your password

## Changing your password when logged in
If needed a user can change their own password in **Employees** -section under **Own info** >**Personal information**.
The password can be changed at the bottom of the page.
The password can be changed as follows:
1\. Enter your current password  
2\. Type in the new password  
3\. Click **Change**.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/33235108678557)
## Ordering a new password
If you have forgotten your current password and can't log in, you can order a new password from your deployment's login page by clicking "**Forgot password** "-link.
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/33235108679453)
In the next phase enter your e-mail address and the verification code, then click Submit. You will receive a link to change the password via e-mail in a short while. 
![](https://support.nepton.com/hc/article_attachments/33235108680861)
If you don't receive the e-mail or you don't know which e-mail address is saved for you in Nepton, please contact your supervisor or your organization's admin user. 
An admin user can also [change user's password](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002430418>).
Notice! If login with Nepton username/email and password has been disallowed, users other than [Administrators](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002425637>) can't set passwords. [More information](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/13194036455325>).
