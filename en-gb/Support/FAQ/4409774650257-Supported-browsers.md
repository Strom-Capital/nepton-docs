# Supported browsers

Nepton service works on all modern browsers. Nepton does active development regarding new browser versions so that the service will always work as well and as fast as possible.
To get the best experience from the Nepton service and to maintain your security, you should always use a modern, actively maintained and developed browser with it's newest product version.
Nepton tracks service usability specially across these browsers:
  * Apple Safari
  * Google Chrome
  * Microsoft Edge
  * Mozilla Firefox
  * Opera


Service does not support Internet Explorer browser. Usage of this browser is not recommended.
Service does not use Flash or Java applets.
