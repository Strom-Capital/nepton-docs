# How to write a good support request?

Please not, that only organization's Nepton-certified Administrators are entitled to use Nepton's Customer Support services. If you are not your organization's trained admin user, please contact your supervisor or your organization's human resources to clarify the matter.
Nepton Customer Service is available on weekdays from 08:00 to 16:00 at asiakaspalvelu@nepton.fi. A support request can also be sent through our [support pages](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4406611917841>) at support.nepton.com (separate registration to the support portal is required).
If you are a certified admin user and about to send a support request to Nepton's Customer Service, please provide us at least the following in your request:
  1. Example person's Nepton employee number (if possible), in whose Nepton calendar the situation occurs (Note! Do not enclose any real persons name in the support request, if possible. Instead, always use some other identification method.)
  2. Example date, when the situation occurs
  3. Description of what happened, what is wrong
  4. Description of how the calculation or situation should be instead
  5. Whether the situation concerns multiple people or just one person
  6. Possible screenshot of the situation (Note! Make sure there is no sensitive information available in the screenshot, blur all the names and other information.)


For example:
  * Example person: provide us the Nepton personnel number (for example 123456)
  * Date: Sunday 9.1.2026
  * Description of the situation: Example person has accumulated 5h of evening bonus on 9.1.2026
  * Description how the situation should go instead: Example person should have accumulated 7h of evening bonus on 9.1.2026


This way, we can provide you with the most straightforward assistance possible in interpreting and resolving the situation in our customer service. Remember, that you may never provide us too many details. Giving us too little information may lead into extra questions, which could be avoided by giving extensive amount of information on your first message. 
If a support request requires submitting confidential material, it must always be sent to Nepton Customer Service via an encrypted connection, and never as a regular email or attachment.
