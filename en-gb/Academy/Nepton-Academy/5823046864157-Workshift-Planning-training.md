# Workshift Planning training

Workshift planning trainings are not held regularly. The training can be booked from customer service, and it will be customized for the customer deployment and needs.
### Who is the training intended for?​
The training is suitable for all admin users and persons who work in the company's management, HR or team lead planning shifts for example. The person can also be a deputy in said tasks. Workshift planning must be in use in the deployment.
We recommend completing the bronze and silver admin level trainings before participating in workshift planning training.
### Skills learned in the training
The training focuses on the prerequisites and specifications for using shift planning, as well as actual shift planning. After the training, the participant understands the principles of workshift planning in Nepton and can perform tasks required in their daily work independently or with the help of the Support articles. S/he knows how to set workshift planning up, what the important settings are and how to plan shifts. Depending on the deployment, the participant will have clear understanding on...:
  * The principles of implementing shift planning
  * The importance of [units](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002590317>), [locations ](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002575538>)and planning levels
  * Creating new[ planning levels](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360005539397>) and [attaching people to them](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002575598>)
  * Management of [competences](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360007613717>) and [tasks](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360010504757>)
  * Settings related to shift planning
  * Shift Reporting​
  * Creation, publication, notification and deletion of shifts and shift requirements
  * Use of shift types, copying of shifts, use of planning models
  * Tasks and competencies helping with shift planning
  * Work shift lists
  * Shift calendars


### How to prepare for the training?
Varmista, että pääset kirjautumaan Nepton ympäristöösi. Katso ohjeesta [Kirjautuminen palveluun](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/fi/articles/360002602998>) kuinka Neptoniin kirjaudutaan.
Please complete bronze and silver level trainings before participating in Workshift Planning training. Make sure you can [log into](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002602998>) your Nepton environment. If the Nepton implementation project is still underway, please contact the Nepton project manager to get credentials for your own environment.
### Signing up for training
Workshift Planning trainings are hold only customized for each customer when requested. Please fill in a form in https://nepton.fi/koulutukset/ or [contact customer support](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/7077076926237>) for arranging gold level training for you.
