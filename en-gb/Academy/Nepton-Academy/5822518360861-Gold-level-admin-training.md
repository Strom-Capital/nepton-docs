# Gold level admin training

Gold level trainings are not held regularly. The training can be booked from customer service, and it will be customized for the customer deployment and needs.
### Who is the training intended for?​
The training is suitable for all admin users and persons who work in the company's management, HR or payroll for example, and whose tasks include integrations. The person can also be a deputy in said tasks. The customer deployment has either scheduled or manual integrations in use.
We recommend completing the bronze and silver level trainings before participating in the gold level training.
### Skills learned in the training
After the training, the participant understands the principles of different integrations between Nepton and other systems. The participants will have understanding in:
  * Integration [functionality](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002489417>)
  * Different types of integrations (SFTP/[API](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360004861518>))
  * [Standardized integrations](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002494538>) and [rulesets](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002492278>)
  * Using integrations both scheduled and manually
  * Using [service log](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002575478>) and [person information change log](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360007566177>) to help in problem situations
  * Using support portal articles as help in everyday work


### How to prepare for the training?
Please complete bronze and silver level trainings before participating in Gold training. Make sure you can log into your Nepton environment. See how to [log in to Nepton](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002602998>) in the instructions.
### Signing up for training
Gold level trainings are hold only customisez for each customer when requested. Please fill in a form in <https://nepton.fi/koulutukset/> or [contact customer support](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/7077076926237>) for arranging gold level training for you.
