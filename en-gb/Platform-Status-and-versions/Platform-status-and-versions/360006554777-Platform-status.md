**Maintenance windows**
The service is updated regularly. The times listed below follow the Finnish time zone ([UTC+2 / UTC+3 during daylight saving time](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_Finland>)). We recommend that scheduled tasks are not arranged during these time windows. Integrations are paused approximately 15 minutes before the start of the release window.
  * Minor changes, fixes and routine maintenance tasks are generally performed on Tuesdays between 19:15 and 19:45. Updates are announced 24 hours in advance.
  * Major feature releases and longer maintenance tasks are primarily carried out on Saturdays, but when necessary also on Sundays, between 03:00 and 05:30. Such updates are announced 2 weeks prior to release. Major feature releases typically occur 1-3 times per quarter.
  * Separate security updates are performed between 22:00 and 24:00. These may take place on any day of the week. If a security update or any other update is deemed critical, it may be carried out at a time chosen by the service provider without prior notice.
