# Nepton’s product development roadmap

**Public**  
**Updated 2.6.2026**
In this article, we describe the outlook of Nepton’s product development and the future focus areas. The plans may change due to changes in strategy and the market. We update this article on average once per quarter.
This article provides a general overview of the direction in which the Nepton product is being developed. Release notes and the support portal provide more detailed information on individual features
### **Long-term product development focus**
  * **Improving usability**  
→ Faster and smoother use, especially in large organizations.
  * **Increasing automation and leveraging AI**  
→ Reduce manual work and improve accuracy.


### **Roadmap**
The development items in the _In Progress_ column are the ones we are currently working on; once they are completed, we will take the next items from the column into development.
IN PROGRESS | **NEXT** | **LATER**  
---|---|---  
Weekly resting time improvement | Enhancing a consistent user experience (Standard UX models) | Enhancing Nepton’s partner tools  
| Accessibility verification for Nepton Working Time | Improving the existing notifications management module  
| Improving self‑service capabilities | Development of the new Nepton Touch terminal  
| Improvements to the approval workflow for working time events and absences |
