##  **20.09.2025** release (2.17 - Shift planning)
###  __ New 
  * #### Year targets in shift planning
    * A new Year target setting has been added to the Worktime settings, allowing you to define the year target in hours. 
    * When planning shifts, you can instantly see the hours of scheduled shifts and how well the year target is being met. 
[ref 14491]
  * #### Overhauled messaging features in shift planning
    * Planners and employees can now discuss shift-related matters directly via the overhauled Messages feature. 
    * Shift planners can view all shift reservations and messages in the Messages view, located at the top of the planning view. 
    * Employees can send messages by opening a shift in the Worktime view and typing into the Message field. 
[ref 15399, 15559]
  * #### New permission for assigning shift templates to locations
    * Planners can now configure which templates are available in the locations they have access to. 
    * This right is granted via the permission sets.
[ref 15741]
  * ####  Worktime setting changes are now logged to the service log, including an optional written reason for the change 
Admins can make the written reason mandatory.
[ref 15749]


###  __ Improvements 
  * #### Shift planning usability improvements
    * Shift templates can be configured for multiple days, including support for empty days. They also include all fields related to shifts. 
    * Reservation messages and Shift tasks have dedicated fields
    * A new field, Shift information for planner, has been introduced. It is visible only to planners and helps facilitate collaboration between them. 
    * The functionality of shifts, shift needs, shifts from work schedules, and deviations has been standardised. 
    * The usability of several planning views has been improved and defining view settings is more flexible.
