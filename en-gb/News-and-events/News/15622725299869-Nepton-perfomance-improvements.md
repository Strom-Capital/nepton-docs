# Nepton perfomance improvements

At Saarni Nepton, we are currently focused on improving the performance of the Nepton service. We have recently increased the capacity of the data center where our service operates. We are dedicated to enhancing the performance of the service to accommodate larger user volumes.
Significant updates have been made to the existing hardware infrastructure recently. We are transitioning to more modern technology in the hardware used in the data center. These changes have been ongoing recently and will continue throughout the early part of the year. In the Nepton service, these changes are reflected in improved performance. For example, the calendar opens faster, report generation is quicker, and integrations operate more efficiently.
We will continue with hardware updates throughout the early part of the year. Additionally, our product development team will begin working on themes related to performance and scalability for the upcoming 2.15 release. These changes will further enhance the speed of the service. While these changes will take time, we anticipate the release date to be in March.
With these improvements, the performance of the Nepton service will benefit existing customers, and we will be better prepared to serve our growing customer base.
