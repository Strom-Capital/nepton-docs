# Nepton release on December 11th brings notifiactions and ip-restrictions

On **Thursday, December 11, 2025** , an update will be released for Nepton, introducing several significant new features. Below is a summary of the changes.
**Tip:** Follow our [**Release notes**](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360006615478>) to receive early updates about major releases—up to two weeks in advance. Instructions for following the page can be found [**How to follow an article**](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4406604045585>).
## Notifications
The **Notifications** feature enables automatic email alerts to inform users or supervisors of important time management events—such as entries pending approval. Notifications can be scheduled and customized to fit your organization's needs, ensuring timely communication and relevant message content.
  * **Preconfigured notifications** are available with the **Working Time Pro** service level and higher.
  * **Customizable notifications** are available with the **Working Time Complete** service level.


You can read more about this feature in our [**support article**](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/32259026029213>).
## Defining Allowed IP Addresses
It is now possible to restrict or prevent staff from recording working hours from specific physical locations, networks, or devices. This ensures that work is logged only from approved work sites.
This is a **paid add-on feature**. Please contact your Nepton representative to enable it. More details are available in our [**support article**](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/articles/31928468706845>).
## Real-Time Integrations
Nepton can now support **real-time integrations** with any system via a live data buffer. Previously, this was only available with Populus and Mepco, but now **Custom Export** also supports real-time connectivity. This enables greater flexibility in building real-time connections with other systems.
If your integration needs an update, please contact your **Nepton representative**.
## Other Small Improvements
  * New permission: Edit project details only for own open work events
  * Updated UI: Person Groups view and Forgotten Password view
  * Security enhancements
