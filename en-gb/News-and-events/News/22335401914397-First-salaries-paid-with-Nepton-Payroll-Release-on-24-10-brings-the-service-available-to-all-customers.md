# First salaries paid with Nepton Payroll – Release on 24.10. brings the service available to all customers

The first onboarding projects of the Nepton Payroll service are underway, and the first salaries have already been paid to our customers' employees! This new service integrates worktime and payroll processing into a single system, providing significant benefits to our customers through time savings, reduced manual work, and streamlined processes.
**Release on 24.10.2024**  
On Thursday, 24 October 2024, the Nepton Payroll service will be officially released for all our customers. With this release, a new "Payroll" tab will appear in the Nepton top menu for administrators, allowing easy access to more information about the service. Once the service is activated, the tab will take the user to Nepton's comprehensive and user-friendly payroll section.
After the release, the Nepton Payroll service will be actively developed based on feedback from oboarding projects and customers. We will be adding new features that meet payroll specialist's needs and further simplify daily routines. More details about future updates will be provided as autumn progresses. Our goal is to offer a solution that streamlines payroll processes and reduces manual work for payroll specialists.
## What is Nepton Payroll?
Nepton Payroll integrates worktime, payroll, and HR data into one easy-to-use cloud service. Nepton offers the most comprehensive worktime interpretations on the market, enabling efficient and reliable payroll processes. The manual work of the payroll specialist is reduced as worktime is accurately interpreted and converted into payroll automatically.
Employees can see their payslip forming as they record their worktime, and they can identify any potential errors before payday. Correcting worktime entries is straightforward for both managers and employees, so issues do not accumulate after payroll. This simplifies the work of the payroll specialist and reduces errors!
Automated payroll can be easily managed in-house or used in together with an external payroll provider. Our Nepton Payroll partners offer comprehensive payroll and accounting services. You can also use Nepton Payroll alongside your current accounting firm or payroll provider.
Implementing Nepton Payroll is easy and comes with minimal risk. We can add the service seamlessly to the time management and compare it to your current payroll processes. **Nepton Payroll is a paid add-on service available to all Nepton customers. Contact your key account manager to learn more.**
