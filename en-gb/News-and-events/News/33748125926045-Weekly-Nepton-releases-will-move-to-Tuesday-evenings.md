# Weekly Nepton releases will move to Tuesday evenings

We are moving our weekly maintenance window to a new time slot. Starting **10 March 2026** , the maintenance window will take place on **Tuesdays from 19:15 to 19:45**. The previous Thursday window (13:00–13:30) will no longer be in use.
This change helps reduce interruptions during office hours and ensures a smoother user experience for our customers. More extensive updates will continue to be deployed during the early Saturday morning maintenance window.
You can find more information about maintenance windows on [the Platform Status page](<https://support.nepton.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360006554777-Platform-status>).
